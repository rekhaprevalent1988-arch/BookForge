FEATURED STORY

Power Within Lightning

The beginning of a larger story.

⚡ The idea

The story begins with a simple but powerful question: what would happen if an ordinary boy suddenly gained the power of lightning?

From that idea grew a world filled with mystery, danger, extraordinary abilities and difficult choices.

🌩️ The journey

Nimit's journey is not simply about having power. It is about learning what power means, understanding its consequences and facing the people who stand against him.

The story builds toward a much larger conflict.

Why I created it:

I wanted to create an exciting story around a power that feels visually spectacular while still giving the main character a meaningful journey.

Magical Keystones

🔮 The world

Magical Keystones is built around the idea of mysterious objects that hold extraordinary importance.

The keystones are more than ordinary objects. Their existence creates questions about power, history and the forces surrounding the characters.

02

03

06

The book

WHAT I LEARNED

Writing a book teaches you more than writing

✍️ Writing teaches patience

A book is not normally created in one sitting. Ideas need time to become characters, chapters and complete stories.

🧠 Problems are part of the process

Writers encounter plot problems, formatting problems, design problems and countless small decisions. Learning to solve them is part of becoming better.

🎨 Books are creative projects

Writing is only one part. Cover design, formatting, editing, publishing and presentation all affect the final result.

🚀 Finish what you start

An unfinished idea stays an idea. Completing the manuscript is what turns imagination into something real.
