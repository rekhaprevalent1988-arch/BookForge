BEFORE YOU UPLOAD

Make sure your book is actually ready

Publishing should be the final stage, not the first time you discover mistakes.

📖 Manuscript checklist

  • Complete story or book content
  • Spelling checked
  • Grammar checked
  • Character names consistent
  • Chapter order correct
  • Headings consistent
  • Page breaks checked
  • Repeated or missing paragraphs removed
  • Final ending checked
  • Final file saved separately

🖼️ Cover checklist

  • Correct title
  • Correct author name
  • Readable typography
  • Good contrast
  • Genre is visually clear
  • No accidental spelling mistakes
  • Correct dimensions
  • Spine included when required
  • Back cover checked for print
  • Image licences checked

METADATA

Your book's information matters

Metadata is the information readers and publishing platforms use to understand and display your book.

Title

Use the exact title that appears on your cover and title page.

Subtitle

Optional. Use it when it genuinely adds useful information.

Author

Enter your author name consistently throughout the book.

Description

Write a compelling description that tells readers what they can expect without giving away everything.

Categories

Choose categories that genuinely match the book.

Keywords

Think about words a reader might actually use when searching for your type of book.

BOOK DESCRIPTION

A simple description formula

1

Introduce the situation

Who is the main character and what is their normal world?

2

Introduce the problem

What changes and creates the central conflict?

3

Raise the stakes

What could happen if the character fails?

4

Finish with curiosity

Give readers a reason to want to discover what happens next.

Example structure:

Aarav thought his life was ordinary until a mysterious message appeared on his desk. It pointed him toward a secret that nobody in his town wanted him to discover. As the clues begin connecting, Aarav realizes that finding the truth could put everything he loves at risk.

Notice: the example creates curiosity without explaining the entire story.

ISBN

Do you need an ISBN?

An ISBN identifies a specific book edition or format. Whether you need one depends on your publishing route and edition.

🇮🇳 For authors in India

India has an official ISBN agency/application system. Before applying, check the current requirements and instructions on the official government website.

Visit official ISBN India website →

Important ISBN rule

Treat different formats and editions carefully. Do not assume that one ISBN automatically covers every version of a book.

Always follow the ISBN agency and publishing platform's current instructions.

PROOF YOUR BOOK

Never skip the final visual check

A manuscript can look perfect on your computer and still have formatting problems after conversion or upload.

Page 1

Check margins, title placement and overall alignment.

Chapter pages

Check chapter headings and page breaks.

Image pages

Check image position, quality and cropping.

Headers & footers

Make sure they are consistent and not cut off.

Page numbers

Check numbering and placement.

Last page

Make sure the ending, author note and back matter are correct.

⭐ Best practice: If your publishing platform allows a proof copy, consider ordering one before ordering or promoting a large quantity.
PRICING

How should you think about your book's price?

Think about these factors

  • Page count
  • Book size
  • Black-and-white or colour interior
  • Paperback or hardcover
  • Printing/production cost
  • Platform fees or margins
  • Distribution
  • Target audience
  • Comparable books
  • Your desired earnings

Don't choose a price randomly

A very low price is not automatically better. Your price must leave enough room for production and platform costs while still being reasonable for your readers.

Use the publishing platform's current pricing or royalty calculator where available.
