01 — WRITING

Tools for writing your manuscript

Choose a writing environment that makes it easy to draft, organise and revise your book.

📝 Google Docs

A simple browser-based writing environment. It is especially useful if you want your manuscript accessible across devices.

How to use it

  1. Open Google Docs.
  2. Create a blank document.
  3. Give it a clear name such as My Novel — Master Manuscript.
  4. Write your chapters in the same document.
  5. Use heading styles for chapter titles.
  6. Use page breaks between chapters instead of repeatedly pressing Enter.
  7. Keep a separate backup copy of your manuscript.
Open Google Docs →

📘 Microsoft Word

A widely used word processor that is useful for manuscript writing and professional document formatting.

Useful features

  • Heading styles
  • Page breaks
  • Automatic page numbers
  • Table of contents
  • Find and replace
  • PDF export
  • DOCX support
Visit Microsoft Word →

📚 Calibre

A free, open-source e-book management application that can also convert between many e-book formats.

Basic conversion workflow

  1. Install Calibre from its official website.
  2. Add your book to the library.
  3. Select the book.
  4. Choose Convert books.
  5. Select your desired output format.
  6. Review the conversion settings.
  7. Convert and inspect the result.
Visit Calibre →

Calibre's official documentation explains that its conversion system supports many formats, while also noting that conversion is not a substitute for a full e-book editor. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

02 — DESIGN

Free and beginner-friendly design tools

Use these tools to create covers, graphics and other visual materials.

🎨 Canva

Canva has book-cover templates and also allows you to start with a blank design.

How to make a cover

  1. Open Canva's book-cover templates.
  2. Search your genre.
  3. Compare several designs before choosing one.
  4. Replace the sample title.
  5. Replace the author name.
  6. Replace or edit the visual elements.
  7. Adjust colours.
  8. Check the title at small size.
  9. Export your finished design using the format required by your publisher.
Open Canva Book Covers →

Canva currently offers thousands of book-cover templates, including free options, and lets users customise text, fonts, colours and images. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

✨ Adobe Express

A browser-based design tool with book-cover templates and a blank-canvas workflow.

How to use it

  1. Open the book-cover maker.
  2. Search for a suitable template.
  3. Replace the sample text.
  4. Add your own images if needed.
  5. Adjust fonts and colours.
  6. Keep the cover visually simple.
  7. Download the finished cover.
Open Adobe Express →

Adobe currently describes its book-cover maker as free to use and provides templates, image resources and customisation tools. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

📐 KDP Cover Calculator

Use this when preparing a print cover for Amazon KDP. Don't guess your spine width.

How to use it

  1. Select your binding.
  2. Select your interior and paper choices.
  3. Select the trim size.
  4. Enter your final page count.
  5. Generate the template.
  6. Use the generated dimensions when creating your cover.
Open KDP Cover Calculator →
03 — IMAGES

Find images for your designs

Always check the licence and usage restrictions for the particular image and source before publishing.

📷 Pexels

Useful for finding photographs and videos for creative projects.

How to use it

  1. Search for the subject or mood of your book.
  2. Open an image you like.
  3. Read the current licence information.
  4. Download the image.
  5. Import it into your design software.
  6. Modify it as necessary for your design.
Visit Pexels →

Pexels says its photos and videos can be downloaded and used for free, including commercial use, under the Pexels licence. It also lists restrictions, so read the licence before publishing. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

📸 Unsplash

A popular source of photography for creative projects.

How to use it

  1. Search for a subject, atmosphere or visual concept.
  2. Look for an image with enough empty space for your title.
  3. Review the current licence information.
  4. Download the image.
  5. Add it to your cover or design.
Visit Unsplash →

Unsplash maintains an official licence guide covering commercial use, attribution, releases and other issues. Check the current licence before using an image in a published product. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}

🔎 Openverse

Useful when you want to search across openly licensed or public-domain media.

How to use it

  1. Search for the subject you need.
  2. Open an individual result.
  3. Check its specific licence.
  4. Follow any attribution requirements.
  5. Save the licence information with your project records.
Visit Openverse →

05 — EDITING

Check your writing before publishing

Software can catch some mistakes, but human proofreading is still important.

📝 LanguageTool

A grammar and spelling checking service that can help identify possible problems in your writing.

How to use it

  1. Open the LanguageTool website.
  2. Paste a section of your manuscript.
  3. Review the suggestions.
  4. Do not blindly accept every suggestion.
  5. Decide whether each change actually fits your meaning and writing style.
  6. Read the final passage yourself.
Open LanguageTool →

✂️ Four Editing Passes

Pass 1 — Story

Does the plot make sense? Are scenes in the right order?

Pass 2 — Character

Do characters behave consistently? Do their choices make sense?

Pass 3 — Language

Improve awkward sentences, repetition and unnecessary words.

Pass 4 — Proofreading

Check spelling, punctuation, formatting and final page errors.

07 — COLOUR

Build a professional colour palette

🎨 Coolors

Generate and explore colour palettes for your cover, website and promotional designs.

How to use it

  1. Open the palette generator.
  2. Generate a palette.
  3. Lock colours you like.
  4. Generate new combinations around them.
  5. Record the HEX codes of your final colours.
  6. Reuse the same palette throughout your visual identity.
Open Coolors →

Simple Example

Imagine your book is a mystery novel.

Instead of using ten unrelated colours, choose a small palette and use one accent colour to draw attention to important elements.

08 — PUBLISHING

Platforms for publishing your finished book

Publishing terms, pricing, availability and requirements can change, so always read the platform's current instructions before uploading.

📚 Amazon Kindle Direct Publishing

Amazon's self-publishing platform for eBooks and print books.

Basic workflow

  1. Create or sign into your KDP account.
  2. Prepare your manuscript and cover.
  3. Enter your book details.
  4. Upload the manuscript and cover files.
  5. Review the preview carefully.
  6. Complete the publishing setup.
Open Amazon KDP →

KDP's official publishing workflow starts with preparing the manuscript and cover, followed by entering book details, uploading files and completing publication settings. :contentReference[oaicite:6]{index=6}

🇮🇳 Pothi

An India-focused self-publishing and print-on-demand platform.

Basic workflow

  1. Prepare your manuscript.
  2. Format the interior.
  3. Design the cover.
  4. Upload the book.
  5. Review the book information.
  6. Set up selling/distribution options available to you.
Open Pothi →

Pothi currently describes a workflow covering editing, interior formatting, cover design, uploading, selling and distribution, with print-on-demand options. :contentReference[oaicite:7]{index=7}
