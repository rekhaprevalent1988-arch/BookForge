THE BIG PICTURE

A book is more than just writing.

A finished book is created through several connected stages. This website takes you through each one.

01

Imagine

Find your idea, genre, theme and the central question your story wants to answer.

02

Build

Create memorable characters, locations, conflicts, goals and a strong story structure.

03

Write

Turn your plan into chapters, scenes and dialogue while developing your own writing voice.

04

Polish

Edit your manuscript for clarity, grammar, consistency, pacing and readability.

05

Design

Create the cover and format the inside of your book so everything looks intentional and professional.

06

Publish

Prepare your files, choose a publishing route, upload your book and make it available to readers.
