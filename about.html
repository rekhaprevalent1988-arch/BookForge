MY JOURNEY

From imagination to creation.

The process behind BookForge can be thought of as a series of small steps.

01

Imagine

Start with an idea. It can be a character, a question, a mystery, a world or simply a “what if?”

02

Explore

Ask questions about the idea. Who is involved? What could happen? What could go wrong?

03

Build

Create characters, locations, conflicts, goals and the rules of your story world.

04

Write

Turn your plan into chapters and scenes. Write the first draft without trying to make every sentence perfect.

05

Refine

Edit, proofread, improve the structure and make the story clearer and stronger.

06

Create

Format the interior, design the cover and prepare the files for publication.

07

Publish

Choose a publishing route, upload the files, preview the book and complete the final steps.

08

Share

Your finished book can now be presented to readers, friends, family and the wider world.

PRINCIPLE 01

Start simple.

You don't need to know how to write a perfect novel before writing your first paragraph.

Start with an idea and improve it step by step.

PRINCIPLE 02

Learn by doing.

Reading about writing helps, but actually creating characters, scenes, chapters and books teaches you much more.

That's why BookForge includes practical exercises and examples.

PRINCIPLE 03

Mistakes are normal.

Your first draft may not be perfect. Your first cover may not be perfect either.

What matters is learning, improving and continuing.

PRINCIPLE 04

Finish the journey.

A great idea is exciting, but a completed book is something you can actually hold, read and share.

BookForge is designed to help you reach that finish line.
